About me

Hello welcome to my page! My name is Lutfy Said. I'm originally from Zanzibar

Hakuna Matata (There's no worries) is our famous Swahili saying, made popular by The Lion King.

Our most famous celebrity to come from Zanzibar was Freddie Mercury. My favourite things about Zanzibar are:

  • Tropical beaches and resorts
  • Slow pace life
  • Friendly, welcoming nature to tourists (Karibu Zanzibar - A popular song welcoming visitors)

I was lucky to have been raised in Tramore Waterford, Ireland at a very young age. A beautiful town next to the seaside (Just like Zanzibar (Minus the weather :D)).

Tramore was a popular holiday destination before the recession for Europeans, Northern/Republic Irish citizens in General.

In the Summers of 2000-2008, you wouldn't be surprised if we had an extra 10,000 tourist, coming to enjoy our town with their families and friends.

Just like Zanzibar, the constant influx of tourists creates an exciting atmosphere.

I'm 35 years old and I am currently working in Infosys Ltd as I.T Support.
